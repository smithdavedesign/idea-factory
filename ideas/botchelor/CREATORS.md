# Creator outreach: draft (2026-11-23)

**PARKED 2026-11-23: David decided not to spend the $250 ("I won't spend that").** Keep this and CREATORS-RESEARCH.md for later; a free version (no fee) is the only one to try until he says otherwise.

The creator list comes from `CREATORS-RESEARCH.md`, which a research agent is filling in. These are the plan and the
messages. David sends every message and pays every creator. Agents don't contact or pay anyone.

## The test
- **Question:** can video from someone else's audience bring visitors who make a character? Our own Shorts, Reels and
  TikTok posts haven't.
- **Budget:** about $250 from David's owner budget (the cap is $300 a product). That buys 3–4 paid videos at $50–80
  each. Leave about $30 spare for a second video from whoever performs best.
- **Who:** small creators with about 5k–50k followers whose audience makes or ships original characters, or who review
  AI character apps. Pick from the research table: 2 in AI apps or Character.AI alternatives, and 1–2 in OCs or
  shipping.
- **What counts:**
  - Each creator gets their own link (below). We count visits, characters made and dates started from each one, using
    our first-party analytics (`utm_source=creator-<handle>`).
  - A creator is worth a second video if their link brings at least 20 visitors and 3 characters made in 7 days.
  - If none of them reach that, video is not the channel. Stop, and keep the rest of the budget.
- **Timing:** the Character.AI auction is 2026-12-01. A "saving your C.AI characters" video is most useful from now to
  about 12-05. An "I put my OC on a blind date" video works any time.

## Rules for every deal
- **Disclosure:** the video must say it's paid ("#ad" or the platform's paid-partnership label). FTC rules require
  it, and so do TikTok and Instagram. Don't ask anyone to hide it.
- **Honesty:** no script that claims what isn't true. Creators use their own words and can say what they didn't like.
  The facts they get are the short list below, taken from MARKETING-FACTS.md.
- **Content:** PG show only. Never After Dark, and no creator whose audience is mostly under 18.
- **About Character.AI:** quote only what it has said, with its date. Never say it is shutting down, and never gloat.
- **Payment:** half up front, half when the video is live, through a platform they already use. David pays. No
  payment for views, follows or comments; no engagement pods.

## Facts they can use
- Botchelor is a blind-date show for original characters. You describe your character in a sentence, they're matched
  with someone, and the date plays out as an episode you watch. Afterwards your character texts you about it.
- You don't steer the date. A rules engine decides the ending (second date, just friends, disaster…), and nobody,
  including us, knows how it'll go.
- 3 free dates a week, no account needed. Companion chat is 30 free replies a day; the Companion Plan is $9.99/month.
- December is "The Botchelor": one lead (Nell, a museum night guard), up to five suitors picked from people's
  characters by audience votes, and a rose ceremony every Sunday. Casting: botchelor.com/december.
- Bring a character from Character.AI at botchelor.com/import. Free tools include ship name generator, random ship
  generator, ship compatibility test, and OC and place name generators.
- Honest cons: PG only, no NSFW. You watch the date, you don't play it. It's new, and the dating pool is mostly the
  show's own cast.
