Checked 2026-11-23. Read-only: public pages only, nobody contacted, nothing signed up for.

**How it was checked**
- **YouTube:** subscriber counts, About text and links come from each channel's public About page. Upload dates come from video pages. "Paid-promotion flag" means YouTube's own "Includes paid promotion" label.
- **TikTok:** follower counts and bios come from the public profile page. TikTok hides a logged-out viewer's video grid, so the activity date is the newest video found through TikTok's public Discover pages, which proves "at least this recent". The newest post may be later.
- **Instagram:** profiles were read through the public web page.
- **Not checked:** emails were copied exactly as published, never guessed. "Email behind button" means YouTube's "View email address" button, which needs a signed-in viewer, so the address was not read.

**Audience-age warning.** Botchelor has an 18+ gate, and the Character.AI and OC-art audience skews young. Every creator below is either an adult by their own bio or shows no sign of being a minor. Several OC-art TikTokers found in the sweep stated ages of 14 to 17 and were left out. Before paying anyone, look at their comment section for a teen-heavy audience.

| [Later, "Influencer pricing benchmarks: 2026 guide"](https://later.com/blog/influencer-pricing-benchmarks-the-complete-2026-guide/) (2026-07-07) | Nano 1K–10K / Micro 10K–100K | $200–500 / $800–2,500 | Feed $100–300 / $500–1,500. Reels add 20–30% | Shorts "in the lower range", like TikTok |

These are agency and platform benchmarks with no audited data behind them. They lean toward US lifestyle creators and run high for niche channels.

**What this means for a $250 test**
- **Price by views.** Modash's CPM rule gives $10–15 per 1,000 views on a small YouTube channel. AIGrabbing's videos get about 3K–10K views, which works out to roughly **$50–150**. MoeMoe's range from 5K to 28K views, roughly **$75–400**.
- **What $250 buys.** At $50–100 a video it buys 2–3 placements from the smallest channels (AIGrabbing, CAI News, maybe MoeMoe). Most 15K–50K creators will quote above $250 for a single dedicated video.
- **What to offer for $50–100.** Offer a 30–60 s integration or a Short, not a dedicated video, and add a ref-link rev-share so the creator has upside.
- **Disclosure.** MoeMoe and AIGrabbing ran sponsor links without YouTube's paid-promotion flag. Botchelor's offer should require the flag or "#ad", because the FTC applies to the brand as well.

**The model these point to.** The common pattern for small AI-app sponsors is **a flat fee per video plus a tracked ref link or code**, sometimes with a time-limited rev share. Rev share runs about 15–30% for SFW and roleplay apps and about 40% for NSFW apps. For Botchelor, that means:
- a flat $50–100 per integration;
- a `?ref=<handle>` link, checking first that the site attributes ref sign-ups and purchases;
- optionally, 25% of referred revenue for 6 months, so a creator whose video works earns more without a bigger upfront spend.
