# Botchelor Growth & Marketing Strategy (/bin/zsh Playbook)

## Core Philosophy
Standard growth tactics (cold emails, DMs, paid ads) fail for consumer entertainment. People don't buy "AI dating sims"—they get hooked on **specific characters, dramatic storylines, and absurd moments** they want to share.

---

## 1. Programmatic "Drama Clips" (Short-Form Video Engine)
- **Concept:** Automatically extract the funniest, most chaotic, or hyper-specific dialogue clashes from Botchelor simulation dates.
- **Execution:** Pipe transcripts into vertical video generators / templates and publish short clips to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels.
- **Hook:** Viewers watch the absurd interaction without signing up, then click through via a QR code or link to see how the full date played out.

---

## 2. Viral "Shareable Moments" (Product-Led Growth Loop)
- **Concept:** Make the output of a date too good not to share.
- **Execution:** At the end of every simulated date, generate a clean, beautifully formatted mobile-optimized "Date Report Card" or "Dealbreaker Breakdown" image.
- **Hook:** Include a subtle watermark and a direct link back to that exact match, turning every active user into an organic acquisition channel when they post to X/Twitter or Reddit.

---

## 3. Community Narrative Drops (Storytelling over Spam)
- **Concept:** Avoid generic "Check out my app" posts (which trigger moderator spam filters).
- **Execution:** Share the funniest character clashes and lore as native text stories or image carousels in communities that love character AI and absurdist humor.
- **Hook:** Frame the post purely around the *characters and stories*, letting interest in the platform follow naturally.

---

## What to Avoid
- **Paid Ads:** Too early; save ad spend until user retention and onboarding loops are locked down.
- **Cold Outreach:** Low conversion for consumer entertainment apps.
