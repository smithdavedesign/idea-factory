# Scribblebox — PRD

## Problem
A parent or grandparent holds a child's drawing they adore. Keeping the paper clutters the house (r/declutter threads), photographing it leaves it in a camera roll, and turning it into a real keepsake costs $18–$100+ and takes weeks (Budsies ~$99, Artkive $75, keyrings £18–£20). There is no instant, cheap, funny option, and holiday gift season is when relatives look for one.

## Target user
The adult who gifts: a parent making a gift for grandparents, or a grandparent/aunt/uncle holding a drawing and wanting something finished by a deadline. Painful moment: the week before a holiday or birthday with a drawing in hand and no idea what to do with it that costs under $15 and arrives today.

## Competitors & gap
Budsies, Artkive and Not On The High Street sellers are physical, slow and $18–$100+. Etsy mockup templates ($1.49–$4.24) are for designers. Canva is free but DIY. Gap: a finished, personalised, instant result at about $9, with the kid's art as the star ("official toy box", "cereal box", "book cover") and a print-ready file.

## MVP scope
- Upload a photo of a drawing; auto crop, straighten and remove the paper background.
- Choose one of 3 templates: toy box, cereal box, book cover (fictional brands only).
- Generated funny blurb from child's name, age and a one-line description; editable.
- Free watermarked preview image; $9 removes the watermark and gives PNG plus 300 DPI print-ready PDF.
- $19 bundle: all 3 templates plus a papercraft fold-out box PDF.
- Share link that opens only the finished image (no gallery, no accounts); files auto-delete after 7 days.
- Email delivery of the download link (no account needed).

## Non-goals
- Shipping physical goods or print-on-demand fulfilment in M1.
- Accounts, galleries or any social features for children.
- AI redrawing or "improving" the child's art; the drawing is reproduced faithfully.
- Real brand names or logos.

## Success metrics
- Preview-to-purchase conversion of at least 3% (below 1% after 300 previews means stop).
- At least 20 paid orders in the first 6 weeks (about $180–$380) from parent/grandparent community posts.
- Median time from upload to finished preview under 30 seconds.
- Under 5% of previews fail background removal and need manual retry.
- At least 15% of buyers share the finished image (viral signal).

## Open questions
- Will anyone pay $9 for a digital mock-up when Canva is free? Untested.
- How well does background removal handle crayon on coloured paper and pencil sketches?
- Best channels: Pinterest and search for "kids drawing gift grandparents", parent communities, or school-year-end posts?
- Is a print upsell (via a print-on-demand API) worth adding after demand shows?
- COPPA and photo-of-minor handling: confirm the buyer-is-adult framing and retention policy.
