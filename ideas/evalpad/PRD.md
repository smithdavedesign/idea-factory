# evalpad — PRD

## Problem
Homeschool evaluators in portfolio-review states receive many family portfolios in a short window before district deadlines. Each arrives as a different mix of photos, PDFs and emails, and each needs a written narrative and a state-format certification. Parents also struggle to assemble the portfolio (see RESEARCH.md). The evaluator's time per family is unmeasured; this must be validated.

## Target user
An independent evaluator (certified teacher or equivalent) in Pennsylvania or Florida first, who reviews 20 or more families a year and is paid per child ($30–$150).

## Competitors & gap
HomeschoolFox ($12/mo) and Easy Homeschool Reporter ($59) serve parents and produce letters from the family's side. No tool found is built around the evaluator receiving, reviewing and signing. The gap is the evaluator's workspace. HomeschoolFox adding an evaluator view is the main threat.

## MVP scope
- Family upload link per child with no account, grouping work samples by subject
- Evaluator inbox listing each family with a status (waiting, ready to review, signed)
- Sorted portfolio view and a one-click combined PDF
- Evaluator notes box, then a draft narrative in the evaluator's wording that they edit
- Pennsylvania and Florida certification letter templates filled from the data
- Automatic deletion of a family's files 90 days after signing

## Non-goals
- Parent record-keeping, planners, transcripts or curriculum
- Payments between family and evaluator
- Any grading of children, or signing on the evaluator's behalf
- States beyond Pennsylvania and Florida in M1

## Success metrics
- 5 of 15 contacted evaluators agree to try it on a real family
- Median evaluator time per family falls at least 30% against their own baseline over 3 reviews
- 3 evaluators pay $19/mo after a 30-day trial
- Letters match the state template in 100% of test cases

## Open questions
- How many hours per family do evaluators spend now?
- Will evaluators trust AI-drafted narratives, and do districts accept them?
- Do evaluators prefer to ask families for files in their own way?
- Can the evaluator pool support a business (total evaluators per state)?
