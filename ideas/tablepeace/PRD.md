# PRD — tablepeace

## Problem
Who can't sit near whom is knowledge held by parents, grandparents and the wedding party, not the couple. Gathering it means awkward calls, and the couple then enters it alone into a solver or onto sticky notes (see WeddingWire threads in RESEARCH.md).

## Target user
An engaged person (or a parent/planner acting for them) with 80–200 guests, 3–6 weeks before the wedding, after RSVPs. They already search for "wedding seating chart maker" and post in WeddingWire and wedding-planning communities.

## Competitors & gap
PerfectTablePlan ($29.95 desktop), the Payhip Seating Conflict Solver ($19–$29 offline browser app, tables only), Table Tailor (weakly rated iOS), Seatbee and Zola (free). All are single-user: the couple types every relationship. Gap: private multi-contributor input from family members, a phone-first flow, and plain-language explanations of unresolved conflicts.

## MVP scope
- Paste or upload a guest list (name, optional party/group) and set table count and capacity.
- Couple creates "contributor links" (e.g. Mom, Dad, Maid of Honor); each link lets that person tag pairs as "keep apart" or "seat together" and add a one-line reason, seeing no one else's notes.
- Couple sees all submitted pairs with the submitter and can accept, reject or mark must/prefer.
- One-click auto-seat to tables with a score and a list of unmet preferences with a suggested relaxation.
- Manual drag/lock then re-solve unlocked guests.
- Print-ready PDF table list and place-card CSV.
- Free to build the chart; $19 one-time unlocks export and contributor links beyond 2.

## Non-goals
Seat-by-seat ordering, floor plan drawing, RSVP/guest management, vendor marketplace, accounts beyond a magic link, native apps.

## Success metrics
- 30 couples create a chart in the first wedding-season month of launch (landing page test); 10% of those pay $19 (3 sales) as the go signal.
- Median time from guest-list import to a full plan under 10 minutes.
- At least 40% of couples who create contributor links get one or more submissions back.
- Solver seats a 150-guest, 30-pair-rule fixture with zero must-avoid violations in under 2 seconds.

## Open questions
