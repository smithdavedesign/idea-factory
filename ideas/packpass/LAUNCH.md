Where to post:
- r/Etsy (https://www.reddit.com/r/Etsy/): Sellers discussing Etsy's EU EPR prompts; ask before promoting.
- r/EtsySellers (https://www.reddit.com/r/EtsySellers/): Active seller community asking about EU compliance.
- Etsy Community forums (https://community.etsy.com/): Existing threads about EPR numbers and shipping to France and Germany.

Post:

Etsy now asks for EPR numbers for France, Germany and Spain, but says sellers have to work out which rules apply. I'm trying to build a simple tool: you answer a few questions about where you ship and it gives you a checklist of what to register, roughly what it costs and what labels you need. It is information only, not legal advice, with sources. Early page: https://idea-pages-livid.vercel.app/packpass/. If you ship to the EU from outside it, what's the part you're most stuck on, and would you pay for a plan or would you rather have someone do the registration?
