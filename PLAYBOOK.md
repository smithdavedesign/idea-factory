# Playbook

What the growth loop has learned across products (RepoHQ `docs/growth-loop-prd.md`). Growth builds and
launch kits get the **Rules** in their brief, and RepoHQ's context search indexes this file. When an
experiment wins, the pipeline adds a line under **Proposed**. David moves it up to Rules, rewords it, or
deletes it.

## Rules

- **Distribution before conversion.** Until a product has 50 visitors a week, its missions bring visitors
  (launch kits, SEO pages), not landing-page or pricing changes: a test on 10 visitors measures nothing.
  (David, 2026-10-12)
- **Honest posts only.** A post is the builder asking a real question. Each community's rules decide whether
  a link goes in; no fake accounts, votes, unsolicited DMs or paid placement. (launch skill)
- **Measure with the product's own beacon.** `visit`, `activate` and `checkout` go to RepoHQ's signal
  endpoint, with no cookies, trackers or paid analytics. (David, 2026-10-12, $0 rule)
- **One change per experiment.** A growth PR changes one thing, so its window's result says what worked.
